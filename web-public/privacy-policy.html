Privacy Policy — Last updated: May 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Lineage Tree ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information when you use the Lineage Tree mobile application.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account information: email address and display name when you register.
  • Family tree data: names, dates of birth/death, gender, life events, and notes you enter for family members.
  • Photos: images you upload for family member profiles, stored in Google Firebase Cloud Storage.
  • Relationship data: parent-child and spouse relationships you define between family members.
  • Usage data: basic analytics to help us improve the app (no advertising identifiers).

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the Lineage Tree service.
  • To allow you to share and collaborate on family trees with invited collaborators.
  • To send important service-related communications (e.g. account security).
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore database and Cloud Storage). Firebase applies industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to your tree data is controlled by security rules — only you and your invited collaborators can read or write your family tree.

5. Data Sharing

Your data is only shared with:

  • Google Firebase — our infrastructure provider (data processor).
  • Collaborators you invite — people you explicitly add to a family tree.

We do not share your data with any other third parties.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data directly within the app.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data (see below).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data.

7. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. To do so, send an email to support@ssemblydev.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address registered to your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your login information — your email address, display name, and authentication credentials will be permanently removed.
  • Family trees you created — any tree where you are the sole owner and there are no other collaborators will be fully deleted, including all people, photos, relationships, and life events within it.

What is not deleted

  • Shared family trees — if a tree you created has other active collaborators, the tree will remain accessible to those collaborators. Your ownership will be removed but the tree data will be preserved so collaborators do not lose their family history.
  • Trees you were invited to — trees owned by other users are not affected by your deletion request.

Send your deletion request to support@ssemblydev.com. Please include your registered email address so we can locate your account. We will process your request within 90 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Lineage Tree is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice.
